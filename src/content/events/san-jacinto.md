    The Homage to the Patron Saint San Jacinto is celebrated on August 15th and 16th in the town of San Jacinto, within the festive calendar of the Tumbes region. The faithful pay tribute to their patron saint with liturgical celebrations, novenas and processions that express the deep popular religiosity of the community.
