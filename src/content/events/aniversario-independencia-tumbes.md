  en: |
    January 7th commemorates the Anniversary of the Declaration of Independence of Tumbes, a foundational milestone in the national narrative: Tumbes proudly holds the distinction of being the first city to emancipate its territory from Spanish colonial rule in 1821. The date carries great political and civic significance in the departmental capital and its districts.

    Local authorities organize a lavish serenade to the city, complemented by civic-military parades, the hoisting of the national flag and an extraordinary fireworks display that lights up the night sky over the boardwalk (malecón) and the emblematic Plaza Mayor. The repetition of this civic rite is vital to cement patriotism in a border area characterized by high migratory permeability.
